Objective

Monterrey lives with heat and poor air quality at the same time, and a monitoring station only describes the spot where it stands. ClimaTec offers an accessible platform with reliable information on the urban climate and air quality within the Distrito Tec.

Hypothesis

Artificial intelligence represents a tool for replicating the physics involved in urban climate and in pollutant dispersion, based on computational fluid dynamics, and represented in an urban digital twin.
